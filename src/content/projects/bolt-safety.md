## Leadership and reach

As Co-Founder, Executive Director, and Chairperson, Vedanshi built and scaled a federally registered nonprofit addressing sexual violence. Its work spans digital resources, workshops, a safe spaces network, documentary film production, and a national safety hotline.

- Reached more than 40,000 people.
- Managed 80+ team members.
- Expanded to 63 Safe Hubs across 20 cities.
- Raised $38,000 in grant funding.

## Partnerships and international impact

Partnerships with L’Oréal Paris, Ted Baker, Guest In Residence, Kulfi Beauty, and NightCap generated 2.2M+ impressions. Humanitarian impact extends across Kenya, India, Colombia, the USA, and Uganda.
