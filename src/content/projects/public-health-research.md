## Berner Lab · Mount Sinai · 2026–2027

Master’s student training in clinical and research methodologies within the Department of Psychiatry. The work includes participant recruitment and screening, psychodiagnostic assessment, fNIRS/fMRI data collection, student coordination, database creation, and data management.

## SHRed Concussions Study · 2022–2023

As a research volunteer with BC Injury Research and Prevention Unit, Vedanshi supported a youth-athlete concussion study funded by the NFL Scientific Advisory Board. Her work included participant follow-ups, hospital-site data collection, and support for the Canadian Injury Prevention Conference.

## Antarctic Institute of Canada · 2021

Co-authored chapters in four publications on human evolution, schizophrenia, and the mental health implications of COVID-19.

## Ye Research Group · UBC Quantum Matter Institute · 2020–2022

Prepared graphene and nanomaterials on substrates to investigate novel optical properties. Co-presented at UBC’s Multidisciplinary Undergraduate Research Conference.
