## Board service and program coordination · 2014–2025

Vedanshi served on the Board of Directors of BC Heritage Fairs Society and coordinated the BC Provincial Heritage Fair in Metro Vancouver.

The board managed charitable assets valued at $85,000+. Vedanshi spearheaded the largest Provincial Heritage Fair in Canada, with recognition and support from the Prime Minister, Lieutenant Governor, Premier, and Mayor.
